About SOBIE

Learn more about our mission, membership, and what SOBIE stands for.

What is SOBIE?

The Society of Business, Industry, and Economics (SOBIE) is a research conference intended to provide a platform for both faculty and students to present their research in a friendly, constructive, and professional setting.

Our Mission

SOBIE’s mission is to provide a platform for the presentation of scholarly research by academicians, students, and business professionals.

Student Involvement

One of the things that makes SOBIE unique is the inclusion of student papers and presentations. SOBIE has always encouraged student participation, and many of our student participants have gone on to graduate school or successful careers in industry and academia.

Membership Benefits

  • Networking with academics and professionals
  • Opportunity to publish in the SOBIE Proceedings
  • Constructive feedback on research
  • Participation in an inclusive and supportive conference

Proceedings and Publications

SOBIE encourages publication of presented papers in its peer-reviewed proceedings. Selected papers may also be considered for journal publication opportunities affiliated with SOBIE.
